# Sales

Captures what customers want and what was sent to them.

## Language

**Customer**:
An external company that orders items from the organization.
_Avoid_: Client, account

**Sales Order**:
A customer's order for one site.

**Sales Order Line**:
One item, quantity, and requested date within a sales order. It is demand.

**Shipment**:
The recorded dispatch of stock to a customer against a sales order line.
_Avoid_: Delivery
