# Quality

Checks conformity and handles what does not conform.

## Language

**Inspection Plan**:
The characteristics to check for an item, with their expected values or tolerances.
_Avoid_: Control plan, checklist

**Characteristic**:
One measurable or visual property checked during an inspection.

**Inspection**:
The record of observed results against an inspection plan for one quantity of stock. It is a receipt inspection or a final inspection.

**Receipt Inspection**:
An inspection of goods received from a supplier.
_Avoid_: Incoming inspection

**Final Inspection**:
An inspection of the output of a manufacturing order.

**Non-Conformity**:
A recorded failure to meet requirements, raised automatically by a failed inspection or manually, for example after a customer complaint. Its disposition is always decided by a person.
_Avoid_: Defect report, NCR

**Disposition**:
The decision on what to do with nonconforming stock: use as is, which makes it available again, or scrap, which writes it off.
_Avoid_: Treatment, decision

**Corrective Action**:
A person-owned action with a due date, addressing the cause of one or more non-conformities. It is open, done, then verified.
