# Documents

Keeps documents and their links to business records.

## Language

**Document**:
A file with immutable versions, linked to business records. Its content is untrusted input.

**Traceability**:
The ability to follow explicit links among receipts, lots, stock movements, manufacturing orders, inspections, documents, and shipments, upstream to origins and downstream to customers.

**Traceability Timeline**:
The chronological view of every linked record for one lot, assembled from the contexts that own them.
