# Lab

Runs and evaluates AI experiments against the industrial core.

## Language

**Experiment Definition**:
A versioned hypothesis with its input and output contracts, baseline, metrics, and allowed tools.

**Experiment Run**:
One execution of an experiment definition against a dataset snapshot and a model configuration.

**Finding**:
A result of an experiment run, linked to its evidence.
_Avoid_: Insight, answer

**Evaluation**:
The comparison of findings with ground truth.
