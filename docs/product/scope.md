# Product scope

## Vision

Industrial AI Lab is a public engineering laboratory for implementing and evaluating AI use cases in realistic industrial workflows. It should demonstrate business relevance, technical architecture, measurable performance, security constraints, and operational limits.

## Users represented

- SME executive
- methods or industrialisation manager
- production manager
- planner
- buyer
- quality engineer
- operator
- IT administrator

These are demo personas, not separate products.

## In scope

- organisations and industrial sites;
- items, units, suppliers, customers, work centres, and machines;
- versioned bills of materials and routings;
- purchase orders and receipts;
- customer demand;
- inventory, locations, lots, serial numbers, reservations, and immutable movements;
- manufacturing orders, operations, consumption, output, and scrap;
- inspections, defects, non-conformities, and corrective actions;
- linked documents and traceability;
- deterministic factory simulation;
- versioned AI experiments, datasets, runs, evidence, and evaluations.

## Explicitly out of scope for the initial product

- accounting, payroll, and HR;
- statutory invoicing;
- complete CRM;
- complete general-purpose ERP configuration;
- advanced finite-capacity scheduling;
- production-ready predictive maintenance;
- SaaS multi-tenancy and billing;
- autonomous agents with unrestricted write access;
- real customer or production data.

## Success criteria

The Lab succeeds when a contributor can reset a scenario, run an experiment against known ground truth, compare it with a baseline, inspect the evidence, and reproduce the result. Feature count is not a success metric.
